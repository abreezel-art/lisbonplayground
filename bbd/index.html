About the workshop

Being Before Doing

It’s about noticing how you are in the moment and acting from it. Through games, movement and emotional exploration, you rediscover your most spontaneous, playful and authentic self.

What can be expected

From applied clown work

Deep contact with oneself and others

  • Deepen your capacity for authentic contact and empathy
  • Intense workout on your most spontaneous self
  • Embrace the power of self acceptance and vulnerability for deeper, more meaningful connections
  • Learn tools to release tension and dissolve resistance
  • Clarify your personal strategies on how you present ourselves to others

Fully embodied emotional work

  • Explore the wisdom in your body, your impulsive, animal and irrational part
  • Integrate both bright and previously difficult parts of your emotional experience
  • Surf and manage bigger emotional waves, opening wider expressive channels to the four basic emotions
  • Align your body-emotions-mind in the same experience and expression
  • Access deeper and bigger pockets of energy and unknown resources in you

Play, creativity and pleasure

  • Celebrate mistakes, being imperfect and taking risks, dissolving the fear of judgement
  • Feel more comfortable in your own skin and less inclined to conform to others' expectations
  • Allow yourself to feel all your own pleasure and utilise humour in relationships
  • Discover a version of personal work that is fuelled by pure fun and aliveness
  • Laugh unapologetically

How it works

Practical, embodied, playful, and honest

1

Action-based learning

Everything is practical and rooted in lived experience, not abstract theory. We work from what is happening in the room and let the real felt experience guide the process.

2

Welcoming mistakes

Transformation requires risk. We learn to meet mistakes, embarrassment, and uncertainty without collapsing into self-judgment, so freedom and creativity can expand.

3

Authenticity and resources

Practices are designed to help you act from a real impulse, respect your boundaries, and stay connected to your sensitivities, instincts, and inner resources even in bigger challenges.

4

Challenge in the yellow zone

We explore the stretch between comfort and overload: enough challenge to grow, but not so much tension that your system shuts down. This is where new choices can appear in real time.

5

Playfulness and fun

Play lowers our defences, increases trust, and lets us experiment without taking ourselves too seriously. It is one of the most powerful ways to create transformation.

About Pedro

Bridging clowning, embodiment, and personal growth

Pedro Fabião is a performing clown, internationally acclaimed trainer, consultant and creative agent. He works at the intersection of clowning, physical theatre, play science and personal development, bringing both artistic depth and practical insight into work with individuals and organisations. His approach is playful, embodied and radically human.
