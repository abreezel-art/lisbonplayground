## Anxiety-Reduction Clarifications

Key concerns to address accurately:

**Experience and ability:**
- "Do I need acting or clowning experience?" → No
- "Do I need to be funny?" → No

**Autonomy and participation:**
- "Will I be forced to do something?" → Participation is invitational
- "Can I set boundaries?" → Yes; boundaries are respected

**The role of the group:**
- "Is there a public audience?" → No external public performance
- "Will people watch me?" → The participant group works together; you may be observed within exercises, but this is part of the shared learning, not performance

## Reasoning

1. **Practical over conceptual:** Answer the visitor's question *What will we actually do?* with concrete activities, not workshop philosophy.

2. **Short and scannable:** Use simple formatting (opening paragraph + bullet list + one clarification) so visitors quickly understand what participation involves.

3. **Free from jargon:** Avoid terms like "patterns of attention," "learning held in relationship," or "integrate." Use plain language: games, movement, exercises, reflection.

4. **Distinction from existing section:** This section is *format and activities*. The existing `method` section explains *how Pedro teaches* (action-based, welcoming mistakes, challenge in yellow zone). They do not overlap.

5. **Accurate on participation:** Honest that the group works together and may observe one another, without using misleading "no audience" language. But clear that this is not public performance.

6. **Clear on consent:** Direct statement about boundaries and "no" without embellishment.
