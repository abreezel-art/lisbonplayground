## How Pedro Teaches

The Second Brain adds detailed material about Pedro’s pedagogy:

- simple rules and progressive constraints;
- practical, action-based exercises;
- attention to body, breath, gaze, and concrete facts;
- converting mistakes and resistance into material;
- asking the audience for precise feedback;
- combining challenge, humour, tenderness, and support;
- naming individual strengths;
- distinguishing observation from projection;
- respecting readiness and no;
- treating the workshop as an experiment rather than a guaranteed breakthrough.

Use the `How Pedro Teaches` files before drafting the **How We Work**, **Meet Pedro**, or relevant FAQ sections.

# Writing Style

Pedro often prefers verbs such as:

- explore
- discover
- notice
- deepen
- clarify
- welcome
- express
- integrate
- celebrate
- connect
- follow
- allow
- listen
- release
- trust

He rarely promises transformation directly.

He invites exploration.

His language often combines:

- embodied specificity;
- emotional honesty;
- humour and irreverence;
- precise observation;
- philosophical depth;
- concrete action;
- permission and challenge.

Avoid generic self-development language, inflated claims, and therapeutic promises.
