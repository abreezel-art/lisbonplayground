# Section 10: Meet Pedro — Proposal

**Status:** Analysis and proposal — not yet implemented  
**Date:** 2026-08-20

---

## Purpose

Build trust by introducing Pedro through his philosophy, presence and way of teaching rather than presenting only a conventional CV.

The section should help the visitor understand:
- Who Pedro is
- Why he teaches this work
- What makes his approach distinctive
- What it may feel like to work with him
- Which credentials are relevant and verifiable

## Relevant Pedro Philosophy and Teaching Material

### From Core Philosophy:

> "The clown lives in the dialogue between the horse and the rider. The horse represents our spontaneity, our impulse, our real deep emotions, our body… the clown, the child energy, pure aliveness. But we also need a rider because we live in society… Clowning is not just wilderness. It's the clash and the dialogue between the horse and the rider."

**Why this matters to "Meet Pedro":** This metaphor is central to his philosophy. It explains why he teaches and what he believes about human potential.

### From How Pedro Teaches:

> "I'm doing this because I want to open this wilderness, this state of spontaneity. So only with your acceptance can I do it. Otherwise it will be pushing or manipulation, and I don't work with that."

**Why this matters:** This reveals his ethical stance and what participants can expect from him. He is transparent about intention and respects consent.

### From Mistakes:

Pedro consistently reframes failure as material and opportunity. His distinctive approach is not to eliminate mistakes but to help people notice them, recover from them, and discover the person revealed in the moment of loss of control.

### From Copywriting Nuggets about Pedro's method:

> "Pedro teaches through close observation. He notices a foot, a delayed beat, a shrinking voice, a face trying to erase a mistake—and turns that tiny event into a precise next experiment."

> "His method combines humour and rigour. He increases difficulty when participants become too comfortable, but explains why he is doing it. He works with resistance as protective intelligence rather than an enemy."

> "He does not reward only technical success. He often celebrates the 'disaster' that reveals a living person."

**Why this matters:** This is a specific portrait of how he works. It distinguishes his approach from generic coaching or therapy.

### From connection and authenticity:

Pedro's work is fundamentally relational. He does not teach people to perform better or hide weakness. He teaches people to remain visible, responsive, and present with others.

## Recommended Structure

### Option 1: CV + philosophy (current approach, expanded)

Structure:
1. Current opening (one paragraph)
2. Why he teaches (mission statement)
3. His approach (how he works)
4. Selected credentials
5. Where to find more information

**Pros:** Linear and traditional  
**Cons:** May feel like expanded CV despite efforts

### Option 2: Philosophy first, credentials as support

Structure:
1. Opening narrative (who he is at the level of practice)
2. What makes his approach distinctive
3. Credentials as evidence

**Pros:** Honors the brief ("philosophy rather than CV")  
**Cons:** Requires smooth transitions

### Option 3: Lead with his effect, then credentials

Structure:
1. What it is like to work with Pedro
2. Why he teaches this way
3. Background and credentials

**Pros:** Emotionally engaging and reader-centered  
**Cons:** Harder to verify, risk of overselling

### Recommendation

**Use Option 2:** Philosophy first, credentials as support. This honors the brief most directly.

### Why he teaches (new section)

> Pedro teaches because he believes that presence, authenticity, and responsiveness are learnable capacities essential to every field—from art to leadership to relationship. He has found that clowning, combined with clear pedagogy and human respect, creates a unique space where people can recover the aliveness and spontaneity that over-control has suppressed. **Why this wording:**

### His approach (new section)

> **How he works**

> Pedro teaches through precision and play. He notices the smallest gesture—a delayed foot, a shrinking voice, the face after a mistake—and turns it into precise, embodied experiments. He combines humour with rigour. He works with resistance as protective intelligence rather than pushing against it.

> His method is built on consent and explanation. He tells you why he is asking for intensity, so you can choose to give it. He does not reward technical success alone—he often celebrates the "disaster" that reveals something true about you. He treats mistakes as material, not failure.

> Most importantly: he remains fully present and human with each person. He does not hide difficulty. He does not perform expertise without including the real person doing the teaching.

**Why this wording:**
- Concrete examples (foot, voice, face after mistake)
- Philosophical grounding (resistance as protective intelligence)
- Explains his ethics (consent and explanation)
- Shows his values (celebrating failure that reveals truth)
- Ends with personal presence (he is not separate from the work)
- Uses Second Brain language that has been verified

## Reasoning

1. **Philosophy before credentials:** The brief asks for introduction "through his philosophy rather than his CV." Leading with mission and method honors this.

2. **Concrete over generic:** Using specific examples (foot, voice, face after mistake) from the Second Brain makes the section distinctive. Any trainer can claim to be "embodied and playful." Pedro's actual teaching is specific.

3. **Transparency about approach:** Explaining his ethics (consent, explanation, treating resistance as protective intelligence) builds trust more than credential accumulation.

4. **Credentials as evidence, not the point:** Biographical facts are included, but in service of the larger picture rather than as the main message.

5. **Honest about limitations:** Avoiding transformation language and universal promises actually builds credibility. He offers method and presence, not guaranteed outcomes.

6. **Accessible to diverse backgrounds:** The section needs to resonate with artists, leaders, therapists, and curious humans. By grounding it in philosophy and method rather than traditional hierarchy, it does.

## Additional Notes

### Tone

The section should feel **personal, warm, and credible** while avoiding:
- A long résumé
- Inflated authority language
- Celebrity positioning
- Vague claims that Pedro is "transformational"
- Presenting him as a therapist unless that description is factually and contextually correct (he is trained in psychodrama, but this is not a therapy workshop)
- Unsupported numbers or credentials

### Length

The proposed section is longer than the current one, but should remain scannable. The use of section headers and white space prevents it from feeling dense.

### Connection to the workshop

The section should make clear that everything described (precision, play, consent, mistakes as material) is what participants will experience in the workshop. It's not just biography; it's a preview of the work.
