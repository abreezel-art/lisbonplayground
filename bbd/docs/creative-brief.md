# Being Before Doing — Creative Brief

> This document is the single source of truth for the landing page redesign.

## Core Goal

The purpose of the landing page is to help the right people recognise themselves, understand Pedro's philosophy, and naturally arrive at the decision to join the workshop.

The page should feel like a thoughtful conversation rather than a traditional sales page.

---

# Emotional Journey

1. Curiosity
2. Recognition
3. Understanding
4. Possibility
5. Trust
6. Evidence
7. Confidence
8. Decision

Each section should answer the next question that naturally arises.

# Pedro Second Brain — Research Layer

The project now includes a structured knowledge base created from workshop transcripts.

Expected location:

`knowledge/pedro-second-brain/`

Use it to deepen and clarify the page while preserving this brief’s structure, emotional journey, and approved direction.

## What the knowledge base may improve

- the specificity of the workshop problem;
- explanations of why clowning and play are used;
- distinctions between Presence, Authenticity, and Connection;
- descriptions of mistakes and emotional work;
- explanations of Pedro’s pedagogy;
- FAQ answers;
- Pedro’s biography through his way of teaching;
- small pieces of language, imagery, and metaphor;
- possible mental-model diagrams.

## What it may not override

- approved section order;
- the emotional journey of the page;
- the protected Hero direction;
- approved concepts;
- real participant testimonials;
- practical facts;
- the quiet, human, editorial tone.

## Content principles drawn from the knowledge base

When relevant, the page may communicate that:

- play is a method, not a decorative theme;
- the body, breath, gaze, and audience are active sources of information;
- mistakes can become material rather than proof of failure;
- authenticity is not performance of a personality but contact with an honest reaction;
- connection is reciprocal and cannot be reduced to private self-expression;
- emotional work is embodied and relational, not a promise of forced catharsis;
- challenge can coexist with tenderness;
- a real no and personal boundaries matter;
- Pedro’s teaching is practical, experimental, precise, humorous, and co-created with participants.

These ideas should be expressed only when supported by Pedro’s original language or the Second Brain.

## Research expectation

Before proposing new copy for a section, Claude Code should consult the relevant Second Brain themes and report which concepts or models informed the proposal.

The knowledge base should make the page more specific, not more dense.
