**Pedro Second Brain (Being Before Doing — June):**
1. **Core Philosophy** — horse and rider model, overdeveloped rider in modern society, need for spaces to exercise spontaneity
2. **State of Play** — play as method, lowering defense mechanisms, making experimentation safe
3. **Presence** — breath, eye contact, body awareness, real-time consciousness, two-way listening
4. **Authenticity** — honest reaction, mask and counter-mask, clown fingerprint, every person fails differently
5. **Connection** — mutual attention, shared release, asking for more contact, one person's mistake releases everyone
6. **Mistakes** — mistakes as material, recovery as training not perfection, mistake as identity vs. information
7. **Emotional Work** — four basic emotions, body-emotions-mind alignment, full embodied expression
8. **How Pedro Teaches** — technical actions over abstract encouragement, letting tension become perceptible, converting failure into material, humour as keeper of intensity, working with resistance as protective intelligence
9. **Mental Models** — horse and rider, mask and counter-mask, clown fingerprint, yellow zone (challenge stress), challenge vs. threat stress
10. **Copywriting Nuggets** — best verbatim quotes, beautiful longer formulations, hero ideas, FAQ concepts, teaching style descriptions
