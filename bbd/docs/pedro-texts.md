Clown - Being before Doing - Clowning in Connection between Inside and Outside

Deep workout in clown presence and state, finding one’s unique way to be in connection with audience, expressing ourselves beyond the control of the mind, opening the channels for clear emotions, discovering both freedom and sensitivity to the audience, exploring the mask (known and integrated elements) and counter-mask (less mature and integrated elements of your personality).

- Rely on your breath, eye contact  and body awareness to guide you through basic and revealing clown improvisations - revealing your personal humour, expressing your most authentic impulses and keeping vulnerable openness and contact with the audience;
- Cut through distorted and mental notions of who you are, what Clown is and find it in the reality of your own authentic experience.
- Reboot your life with the true fun based on Connection, Playfulness and Flow.

3. Description of the 1-day workshop in Bali (the workshop was based on Being Before Doing)

[08.12.2024 17:37] Pedro Fabião: Clown - The Authentic You Beyond All Roles

"The art of the clown consists of freeing ourselves from all roles except one: Our one."

This full day training, led for the first time in Bali by one of the world’s top trainers on the art of Clowning, follows a pedagogy applied to personal growth through play, fully embodied emotional work, and deep contact with oneself and others.

What can be expected from applied Clown work:

Deep contact with oneself and others
 • Deepen your capacity for authentic contact and empathy
 • Intense workout on your most spontaneous self
 • Embrace the power of self acceptance and vulnerability for deeper, more meaningful connections
 • Learn tools to release tension and dissolve resistance
 • Clarify your personal strategies on how you present ourselves to others

Fully embodied emotional work
 • Explore the wisdom in your body, your impulsive, animal and irrational part
 • Integrate both bright and previously difficult parts of your emotional experience
 • Surf and manage bigger emotional waves, opening wider expressive channels to the four basic emotions
 • Align your body-emotions-mind in the same experience and expression
 • Access deeper and bigger pockets of energy and unknown resources in you

Play, Creativity and Pleasure
 • Celebrate mistakes, being imperfect and taking risks, dissolving the fear of judgement
 • Feel more comfortable in your own skin and less inclined to conform to others' expectations
 • Allow yourself to feel all your own pleasure and utilise humour in relationships
 • Discover a version of personal work that is fuelled by pure fun and aliveness
 • Laugh unapologetically

How?

This one day masterclass will be a compact version of Pedro’s acclaimed Being Before Doing workshops.
 It consists in a series of practices and games done in the big group, in couples, and sometimes in solo exercises, where deep contact and emotional release will be the starting points - and with the generous mirroring eyes of our partners and the audience, it will lead to a new experience of ourselves in a heightened state. As the approach is based on the art of clowning, laughter will be omnipresent and sometimes punctuated by the occasional tears.

Pedro’s approach is:

1.  Action based.   All with be practical, drawn from embodied facts and lived situations in the training, and not abstractions, memories or intellectual models of understanding. We will map all the experiences, but privilege the territory to the maps.
      
2.  Welcoming mistakes. Transformation, growth and learning require accepting mistakes. We will approach the inner approach of dealing with mistakes each participant brings, so you can explore, risk and increase your freedom of action.
      
3.  Connecting with your authenticity and resources. Practices that root your experience and action in a real impulse, respecting your boundaries, following your sensitivity and perceptions. Learn how to stay safe in bigger challenges, by distinguishing safety and comfort.  
4.  Challenge-stress’ friendly. Without challenge, there’s no new information - we call it the “no stress” green zone. Also, too much tension leads to overload, with no real growth - it’s the red zone of “threat stress”. Explore the yellow zone - the “challenge stress” state, where your auto-pilot reactions can be brought to light, and you can choose new ones in the heat of reality.
      
5.  Made of playfulness and fun. Play opens us to transformation, by lowering our defence mechanisms, increasing trust and risk taking. Also, it develops an amazing skill for a growth mindset: not taking ourselves so seriously!

4. И вот это мы писали для Lisbon Playground когда создавали проект

**5 Cs Playground**

We are inviting anyone to our monthly sessions using clowning, play and the creative arts to develop a sense of community, connection, creativity, celebration and of course the clown in each of us. See bellow what we mean by that. 

We will meet for 3 hours per month to play together, believing that is the best way to meet and really get to know ourselves and other humans. 

If this appeals to you, be welcome!

**Playground** - the idea is to create a space that is out of daily life. A time that is not productive, not work, not even work on yourself. It’s free time. Time fro free play, where you can follow any inner impulse and experiment with it, not fearing consequences because there are none - all is reversible in the playground, and you don’t have to achieve anything.

**Community** - a big pillar for these sessions is to slowly weave something most of us are missing nowadays - a community of people that are physically there, that are palpable, that you can hug, laugh with, tickle, hit (lightly), look at, cry, touch, do nothing with. It’s a place to meet others, to create bonds.

**Connection** - the glue for this process is genuine connection. We will go through exercises and group dynamics that make it easier to listen to yourself, say yes to what your body and subconscious are saying, connect with all the layers of yourself. Simultaneously, we will connect with others, see and be seen, be supported, validated and sometimes perhaps be challenged in our assumptions. Authentic contact will be the safety net and connect us deeper.

**Creativity** - when our plans and intellect can’t control anymore, we find other intelligences, other resources. We will discover surprising ways by not following the usual ways. We will discover that in everyone there is an endless source of answers and potencial. We will use our bodies, voices, breath, visual imagination, collective images, dreams, everything to enrich our reality. We will unblock our flow.

\*\*Celebration \*\*- whatever we are, whatever appears, we will celebrate it. Cherishing ourselves, the simple fact we exist and breathe and eat and feel is magic and too often we don’t give enough space for appreciation. A toast to the small things!

**Clowning** - the state that the clown in us brings upon is one of full presence, heightened attention, open expression and playful mischief. We will tap into this world of emotions and dreams, this land of big ideals and big falls too. A the party of imperfections and mistakes. We are all fallen angels and primitive monkeys in one body. 

A little excursion for those who are suddenly unfamiliar with Pedro and what he does.

Pedro Fabiao is a clown and director, and has been training hospital clowns (a profession that requires great sensitivity and empathy) and actors all over the world for the past 12 years. Plus, he is a psychologist (psychodramatist). Pedro actively combines these two facets in his work (essentially helping people not just to find and manifest their creativity, but also to gently go through the inner blocks that prevent them from doing so).

A few years ago, Pedro discovered that the tools from clowning and improvisation work in other contexts as well. For example, to develop one’s leadership abilities, capacity for listening and communication, effective team-building, for conflict resolution, it is important that people meet each other with self awareness and flexible social masks. On a personal level, although Pedro’s approach is based on humour, encountering difficult emotions is not uncommon at all - then the big lesson is to find what makes us say yes to the experience and allow for movement and ultimately transformation. 

The secret ingredient is the state of play, which lowers defence mechanisms and increases pleasure, spontaneity, exploratory curiosity and openness to new experiences and to others.

This state of play and ease is what you can take with you from these encounters into your life. And also - really close friends and perhaps, in the future, partners for joint creative projects.

The idea is to gather in Lisbon a community of people interested in welcoming this in their lives, a space where you can safely come with everything you have inside.

Let's play together. And see what's born of it...

5. Transcript of Pedro's promo videos about Being Before Doing

Hi, Lisbon! Hola, Lisboa!

We are getting ready for our next workshop — Being Before Doing — my introduction to clowning. And whatever your idea about clowning is, be ready to be surprised.

What clowning really is about - is connection. With yourself, with others, and with a whole audience. Because audiences don't lie.
 It's being in your maximum authenticity and still feeling totally connected to others.

**What's gonna be happening?**
 In this workshop, we will have a lot of games, exercises, and some deeper practices based on eye contact, breath, voice and body expression. And through these games, you will find that **the most playful you is actually the entrance door to your clown**. Because through play, your clown is going to start popping up. And **the clown in you is you amplified.**

We are also going to **create space for big emotional openings**. We will work with the four basic emotions — joy, fear, anger and sadness — and **you will be able to explore which ones you already have full access to, and which ones are needing a little hello.**

And here's something important: in the games we are going to be playing, there are norms — and there are breaks of the norms. **And humor happens when you fail. So you are going to fail a lot. But you'll enjoy it. And everybody is going to love you.**

See you on the 8th and 9th of June!
